Lossless Recorder Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

1. Controller

Lossless Recorder is provided by Tarek Wasfy. For privacy questions or requests, email tarekwasfy01@gmail.com.

2. Information processed by the app

Lossless Recorder does not require an account and does not include developer-operated advertising, analytics, or telemetry. The app does not send recordings, recording settings, or listening history to the developer.

Audio you choose to record is processed on your device and saved in the output folder you select (by default, the app's Lossless Recorder folder in your Music directory). Recording files and related metadata remain on your device unless you choose to share or move them. The app may store settings and WebView profile data, such as cookies and site storage, locally so embedded websites can work and retain their own session state. You can manage or remove these files using the app's controls where available or your operating system's file and site-data tools.

The app may process track title, artist, album, and cover-art information to name or split recordings and display metadata. When you use the MusicBrainz lookup, the app sends the artist and/or track title you submit to MusicBrainz. It may then request cover art from MusicBrainz's Cover Art Archive. These services receive ordinary connection data, such as your IP address, and process requests under their own policies. If you configure a custom “now playing” metadata URL, the app requests that URL to retrieve track information; its operator can receive connection data and the request. The developer does not operate these services.

3. Embedded music services and audio permissions

The app can display third-party music services, including Deezer, Qobuz, Tidal, and websites you choose. When you open or use one, that service may receive your IP address, browser/device information, activity, and any sign-in information you enter, and may store cookies or similar site data. Those services control their own processing under their privacy notices and terms. The app developer does not receive your service credentials or browsing activity through a developer server.

Depending on your platform and recording source, the operating system may ask for permission to capture audio or access an audio input. Audio capture is used to create the recording you request; the app does not upload captured audio to the developer. Your operating system may separately process permission, crash, licensing, or diagnostic information under its own settings and privacy terms.

4. Retention and your choices

Recordings remain in the output location until you delete or move them. Local app settings and embedded-site data remain on the device until you clear them or remove the relevant app data. Requests to MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, custom metadata URLs, and embedded services occur only when the related feature or website is used. You can avoid those connections by not using those features or websites.

5. Legal basis and data protection rights

Where the GDPR applies, local processing needed to provide the recording and metadata features you choose to use is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Support-message processing is based on responding to your request and, where applicable, taking steps before a contract; proportionate records may be kept on the basis of legitimate interests in providing support (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, or portability where applicable, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact the controller above to exercise your rights. These rights apply to data the developer can access; the developer cannot retrieve or delete information held by third-party services or only on your device.

6. Support messages

If you email the developer, the information you include (such as your email address and message) is used to respond and handle the request. It is retained only as long as needed for that purpose, unless longer retention is required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. Your email provider may process the message under its own terms. Please do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

7. Children and changes

Lossless Recorder is a general-purpose desktop application and is not designed to collect children's personal data. This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date identifies the current version.
