BigTIFF Viewer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Information handled

BigTIFF Viewer has no account, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated upload service. It opens raster files selected by you and reads image pixels and metadata locally on your Mac. The app does not send selected files, image contents, or viewing activity to the developer.

The app includes GDAL and related geospatial libraries. If you open a network-backed dataset or remote resource through a supported source, that service may receive the network information required for the request. The service operator controls its own logging and retention.

Storage and sharing

The app does not operate a developer data store. Files remain at their original locations unless you choose an export, copy, or share action in macOS. macOS backup and storage settings apply.
