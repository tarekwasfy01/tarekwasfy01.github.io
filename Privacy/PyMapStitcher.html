PyMapStitcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Information and local storage

PyMapStitcher has no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking services. The developer does not receive selected map areas, download histories, files, or activity logs. Images are processed locally on your Mac. TIFF output is saved at the location you select. Local settings and error logs may remain in the macOS app container.

Map preview and download requests

The interactive map preview uses the currently selected provider (Google Satellite by default) and sends HTTP(S) tile requests directly to that provider. You can select another included provider or enter a custom XYZ template. The provider receives normal request information, including your IP address and requested tile paths, which can reveal the viewed or downloaded area. The provider controls its own logging and retention. The developer does not operate a relay server.

Children's privacy

PyMapStitcher is a general-purpose mapping tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children.
