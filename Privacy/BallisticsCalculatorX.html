2. Data processed by the app

Ballistics Calculator X does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or developer-operated telemetry. The Windows app processes the values you enter on your device to perform calculations and display results. Local comparison models run on your device.

If you select the P-Max calculation model, or include P-Max in a comparison, the Windows app sends the submitted calculation fields to P-Max over HTTPS and receives the calculation response. Depending on the selected calculation, submitted fields can include powder or manufacturer selection, powder charge or requested maximum pressure, powder space, bullet weight and type, calibre, barrel and case dimensions, and unit preferences. P-Max receives the request and may process technical connection data such as your IP address under its own privacy policy and terms. The app developer does not operate or control that service.

The app may also request graph images from P-Max when displaying P-Max results. Do not submit information you do not want the selected service to receive. Review P-Max's privacy information and terms before using its online model.

The current macOS build is a user-interface shell whose calculation action displays a local placeholder result. It does not currently send calculation inputs to P-Max or another online calculation service. This statement should be updated if a later macOS build adds online calculations or other data processing.

Calculation values and results are held in the app while you use it. The app does not send them to the developer. If you export a graph, the exported image is saved to the location you choose.

3. Safety and intended use

Ballistics Calculator X is a computational tool and does not provide validated ammunition load data or safety recommendations. Its output must not be used to select or verify a charge for a firearm. Consult current published reloading manuals, powder and component manufacturers, and qualified professionals. This notice describes the app's purpose and does not change how data is processed.

4. Network, platform, and diagnostics

Online requests occur only when you use the P-Max model in the Windows app. The request goes directly to P-Max; it is not routed through a developer-operated server. Microsoft may separately process Store, licensing, crash, or diagnostic information under your Windows settings and Microsoft's terms. Apple may separately process App Store or system diagnostics for the macOS app under Apple's settings and terms.

This policy page is hosted with GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process technical request information under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

5. Support messages

If you contact the developer, information you provide, such as your name, contact details, and message, is processed to respond to your request and document its resolution. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for requests concerning a contract or steps before a contract, and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for other support requests. The legitimate interest is providing support and keeping a proportionate record of the response.

Support messages are retained only as long as needed to handle the request, unless longer retention is required by law or needed to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. Email providers involved in delivery may process the message under their own privacy terms. Do not include passwords or sensitive material unless needed for support.

6. Your data protection rights

For personal data the developer holds, you may request access, rectification, erasure, or restriction of processing. Where processing is based on legitimate interests, you may object on grounds relating to your situation. You may also request data portability where Article 20 GDPR applies. Contact the controller above to exercise these rights. You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, including the authority where you live, work, or where an alleged infringement occurred. In North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany, this is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen.

7. Children and changes

Ballistics Calculator X is a general-purpose desktop calculation tool and is not designed to collect children's personal data. This policy may be updated if the app's features or data handling change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
