Maps Downloader Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Information and local storage

Maps Downloader has no account, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated upload service. It does not send download history or the selected map area to the developer. Temporary jobs and results are stored in the app's private macOS container. You can export results through the macOS save dialog and delete temporary jobs in the app.

Map and download requests

When you preview a map or start a download, the app contacts the server selected from a preset or entered URL. The server receives normal HTTP(S) request information, which can include your device's IP address, request path, and protocol metadata. The provider controls its own logging and retention. The app developer does not operate or control those servers.

Sharing and retention

The developer does not receive local files, URLs, coordinates, or activity logs. Temporary files remain on your Mac until you delete them or remove them through macOS storage management. Exported files follow your chosen storage and backup settings.

Children's privacy

Maps Downloader is a general-purpose mapping utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children.
