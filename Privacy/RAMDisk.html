2. Data processed by the app

RAM - Disk creates and manages temporary RAM-backed volumes on your Mac using built-in macOS disk utilities. The app processes the volume name, requested size, selected filesystem, device identifier, and mount point locally to perform the requested operation and show the volume in its list. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or developer-operated telemetry.

The app does not send volume contents, names, sizes, device identifiers, mount points, or activity to the developer. RAM disk contents are volatile and are lost when the volume is ejected or the Mac restarts. Copy out any files you need before ejecting the volume.

macOS receives the disk-management commands needed to create, format, mount, inspect, and eject the volume. The operating system and its built-in utilities process this information under Apple's system policies. The developer does not receive a copy through RAM - Disk.

3. Network and diagnostics

RAM - Disk does not make network requests for its RAM-disk functions. macOS or Apple may process diagnostics or crash information according to your system settings and Apple's privacy terms. Such processing is controlled by Apple, not by the app developer.
