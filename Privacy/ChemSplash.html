2. Data processed by ChemSplash

ChemSplash is a desktop chemical-structure editor. The editor and its resources are included in the app and run locally on your Mac. Chemical structures, document contents, and files that you open or export are processed on your device. The app does not transmit these files or structures to the developer, and the developer has no access to them through the app.

The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or developer-operated telemetry. Interface preferences, such as the selected drawing style and zoom level, may be stored in the app's local WebKit or macOS preferences storage. This data remains on your Mac under your macOS account. The app does not use it to track you across apps or websites.

You choose which files to open and where to save exports using macOS file dialogs. Your files otherwise remain at the locations you choose. Local editor preferences can be removed by deleting ChemSplash's local app data from macOS. Your documents and exports must be deleted separately from their chosen locations.

3. Network connections and external websites

Normal editing uses the copy of the editor bundled in the app and does not require an account or a developer-operated server. If you follow an external website link from the editor, that website may receive standard connection data, such as your IP address, browser information, and the page requested. The operator of that website is responsible for its own processing; its privacy policy applies.

This policy page is hosted with GitHub Pages. When you view it, GitHub may process technical request information under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

macOS and Apple may process app installation, crash, or diagnostic information according to your system settings and Apple's own privacy terms. Such processing is controlled by Apple and is not a data feed to the app developer.

4. Support requests

If you email or write to the developer, the information you provide—such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message—is processed to respond to your request and, where needed, document its resolution.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where the request concerns a contract or steps requested before a contract, and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for other support correspondence. The legitimate interest is handling support requests, maintaining a record of the response, and protecting or pursuing legal claims where necessary. Providing contact information is voluntary, but without a usable reply address the developer may not be able to respond.

Support correspondence is kept only for as long as needed to resolve the request. It may be retained longer where required by law or where necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. Email is transmitted and stored through the email providers used by you and the developer; those providers process the message under their own terms and privacy policies. Do not include passwords, private chemical data, or other sensitive information unless it is necessary for your request.

5. Recipients and international transfers

ChemSplash does not send your structures, opened files, or editor activity to the developer or to an app analytics provider. For support correspondence, the email providers involved receive the message so it can be delivered. A provider may process data in countries outside the European Economic Area under its own privacy terms and transfer safeguards. The developer does not otherwise disclose app data, except where disclosure is required by law.
