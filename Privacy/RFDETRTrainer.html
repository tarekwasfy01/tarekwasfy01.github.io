2. Data processed by the app

RF-DETR Trainer does not require an account and includes no developer-operated advertising, analytics, or telemetry. The app does not send datasets, images, videos, annotations, models, checkpoints, detection results, or exports to the developer.

Images, videos, and datasets you select are processed locally on your device. Training, inference, annotation, SAM 2 segmentation, and exports also run locally. Project files and results are saved where you choose. The app may also store settings, model caches, and runtime data locally. On macOS, the app data directory is in “Library/Application Support/RF-DETR Trainer”; model and library caches are stored there as well. On Windows, storage locations depend on the selected project folder and local app data settings.

The app displays status and error messages while it runs. These may include local file paths or project and model names you selected. Before sending logs or screenshots to support, please remove confidential information.

If your datasets depict people or otherwise contain personal information, you process that information locally as the app user. You are responsible for having an appropriate legal basis, informing affected people, and respecting their rights. The app does not transmit those datasets to the developer.

3. Downloading pretrained models

When you use a pretrained RF-DETR model whose weights are not already available locally, the app requests the model file from storage.googleapis.com. The storage provider receives ordinary connection data, such as your IP address, request time, technical request information, and the requested model path. The model weights are cached on your device. Your training images, videos, datasets, and annotations are not sent with this download.

The download connects your device directly to the model host. Its processing is described in Google Cloud's Privacy Notice. The developer does not operate this service and does not control its logging or retention. Google may process data outside the EU/EEA. Google describes its transfer mechanisms, including EU Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable, in its Cloud Data Processing Addendum, which also provides information about contractual terms and safeguards.

The SAM 2 model included with the app does not need to be downloaded separately for local use.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Article 77 GDPR), in particular in your place of habitual residence, place of work, or the place of an alleged infringement. In North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany, the authority is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia.
