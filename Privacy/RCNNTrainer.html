2. R-CNN Trainer app

Data processed on your Mac

The app lets you select images, videos, annotations, project folders, model checkpoints and export destinations. It processes the selected material locally on your Mac to train or run object-detection and segmentation models, review annotations, and create exports. The app does not send this content to the developer and does not require an account. You decide which files and folders to open and where results are saved.

The app stores its settings and macOS security-scoped file bookmarks in its app-support container so it can remember preferences and reopen locations you authorized. Project files, source data, checkpoints and exports remain at the locations you choose. You can remove those files and the app's local settings/bookmarks from your Mac. The developer has no remote copy to delete.

Optional model-weight download

If you enable the pretrained TorchVision weights option, the app may download the selected public model weights from the download host configured by TorchVision/PyTorch. The host receives ordinary connection data needed to serve the request, which can include your IP address, the requested model file, date and time, and technical request metadata. This optional request does not include your images, videos, annotations, projects or trained models. The host's own privacy terms and retention practices apply. You can leave pretrained weights disabled and use local checkpoints instead.

SAM 2 checkpoints are selected from local storage; the app does not download them. The app contains no account, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated telemetry service. It does not make automated decisions about you or profile you.

Purposes and legal bases

The developer does not receive personal data from files processed locally by the app. For optional model-weight requests, the remote host processes network data to deliver the file; consult that provider's notice for its legal basis and retention.

If you email the developer, the message's sender/contact details and its contents are processed to answer the enquiry and provide requested support. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where the request concerns a contract or steps requested before entering one; otherwise, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, based on the legitimate interest in responding to support and privacy enquiries. You are not required to provide information, but without enough information the developer may be unable to answer.

Recipients, transfers and retention

App files and settings are not shared with the developer. A model-weight host receives the connection data described above only when you request the optional download. The developer does not control that host, its location, transfers or retention; review the host's current privacy documentation before downloading.

Support email is accessible to the developer and the email service provider used to receive it. Messages are retained only as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any related follow-up, and longer only where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. The developer does not sell personal data.
