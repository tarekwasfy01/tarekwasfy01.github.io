Star Map Downloader Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Information handled

Star Map Downloader has no account, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated upload service. The app does not send selected sky areas, output files, or download history to the developer.

Survey requests and local storage

Download requests go directly to the HiPS survey provider selected by you. That provider may receive normal request information, including your device's IP address and requested survey tiles, and controls its own logging and retention. Downloaded images and survey data are stored locally at the output location you select. The developer does not receive or retain the files. macOS storage, backup, and sharing settings apply.
