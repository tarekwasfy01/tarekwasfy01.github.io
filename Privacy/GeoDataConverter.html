GeoData Converter Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Information handled

GeoData Converter has no account, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated upload service. Selected files are processed locally by the app and its bundled GDAL helper. The app does not send local file contents, conversion options, or conversion logs to the developer.

GDAL supports local and network-backed data sources. If you select a remote dataset or a format that contacts a remote service, that service may receive the network information necessary for the request. The service operator controls its own logging and retention.

Storage and sharing

The app writes output to the location you select and may use temporary local files while processing. The developer does not receive or retain these files. You control exported data, backups, and deletion.
