2. Processing files in the app

ISA-L Zip does not require an account and includes no developer-operated advertising, analytics, or telemetry. The app processes the files and folders you choose to browse, compress, inspect, update, test, or extract. File names, paths, archive entries, file contents, and related metadata are handled locally by the app and its compression tools. The app does not send your files, archive contents, passwords, or file lists to the developer.

Archives and extracted or compressed output are stored in the locations you choose. On macOS, the app may store the path to a 7-Zip executable you selected in local app preferences. That setting is not sent to the developer. Status and error messages may include file names or paths; remove confidential details before sharing screenshots or logs with support.

3. Network connections and third-party services

The app does not make background network requests to the developer. On Windows, choosing the Developer link opens the developer's GitHub profile in your default browser. If you follow that link, GitHub receives normal connection information such as your IP address and browser details under its own privacy statement. The app does not transmit archive data when opening this link.

Apple, Microsoft, and the applicable app store or operating system may separately process information related to app installation, licensing, crashes, or diagnostics. Those processes are controlled by the platform provider and your system settings, not by a developer-operated app analytics service.

4. Website hosting

This policy is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process technical connection information such as your IP address, request time, and browser information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. GitHub states that it may process data outside the EU/EEA and generally relies on EU Standard Contractual Clauses for relevant transfers; its privacy statement also describes the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The developer does not use analytics or advertising trackers on this page.

5. Support requests

If you contact the developer, the information you provide—such as your email address, name, message, and any files you choose to attach—is processed to answer and handle your request. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR when your request concerns a contract or pre-contractual steps, and otherwise the legitimate interest in responding to and documenting support requests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Support messages are kept only as long as needed to handle the request, unless a legal retention duty or legal claim requires longer storage. Google/Gmail, as the email provider, may process your message and associated connection data under the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Retention and recipients

Your files and archives remain on your device or chosen storage location until you or your operating system delete them. The developer cannot access or delete them remotely. The developer does not sell personal data or receive archive contents through the app. Potential recipients of technical connection information are GitHub when you visit this policy or choose the Developer link, and Google/Gmail when you email support. Each provider determines its own retention periods and any international data transfers; see the provider notices linked above.

7. Your rights

Where the developer processes personal data as controller, you may exercise the rights provided by the GDPR, including access (Article 15), rectification (Article 16), erasure (Article 17), restriction (Article 18), and data portability (Article 20), subject to the conditions in the GDPR. You may object, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21), and withdraw consent with future effect where processing is based on consent. Contact the controller above to exercise these rights. Requests about data held by GitHub, Google/Gmail, Apple, Microsoft, or another provider should be made directly to that provider.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority under Article 77 GDPR, in particular where you live, work, or where an alleged infringement occurred. In North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany, the authority is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia.

8. Required information and automated decisions

You do not need to provide personal data to the developer to use the app. Support information is voluntary, though a request may not be answerable without enough detail. The developer does not make decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

9. Children and changes

ISA-L Zip is a general-purpose desktop application and is not specifically directed at children. This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices materially change. The effective date identifies the current version.
