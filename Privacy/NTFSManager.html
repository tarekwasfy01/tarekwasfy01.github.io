2. Data handled by the app

NTFS Manager does not require an account and contains no developer-operated advertising, analytics, or telemetry. The app and its FSKit extension process the disk images, volumes, files, folders, and metadata that you choose to open or mount. This processing takes place locally on your Mac. The app does not send your volume contents, file names, paths, or activity to the developer.

If you enable the app's login item, macOS stores and manages that setting. NTFS Manager does not transmit it to the developer. Status and error messages may include file or volume paths; remove confidential details before sharing screenshots or logs with support.

3. Storage and sharing

NTFS Manager has no developer-operated data store. It does not copy or upload your files unless you choose an export or other file operation in the app. Changes made through an enabled writable NTFS mount are written to the selected volume. Your macOS file access, backup, and storage settings continue to apply.

4. Network connections and platform services

The app does not make background network requests to the developer. Apple and macOS may separately process information related to app installation, updates, extension activation, crashes, or diagnostics according to your platform settings and Apple's privacy terms. These services are operated by Apple, not by the developer.

This policy is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process technical connection information such as your IP address, request time, and browser information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The developer does not use analytics or advertising trackers on this page.

5. Support requests

If you contact the developer, the information you provide—such as your email address, name, message, and any files you choose to attach—is processed to answer and handle your request. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR when your request concerns a contract or pre-contractual steps, and otherwise the legitimate interest in responding to and documenting support requests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Support messages are kept only as long as needed to handle the request, unless a legal retention duty or legal claim requires longer storage. Your email provider may process the message and connection data under its own privacy terms.

6. Recipients and retention

The developer does not sell personal data or receive disk contents through the app. Files remain on your device or chosen storage location until you or your operating system delete them; the developer cannot access or delete them remotely. Potential recipients of technical connection information include GitHub when you visit this policy, your email provider when you contact support, and Apple for platform services. Each provider determines its own retention periods and any international data transfers.

7. Your rights

Where the developer processes personal data as controller, you may exercise the rights provided by the GDPR, including access (Article 15), rectification (Article 16), erasure (Article 17), restriction (Article 18), and data portability (Article 20), subject to the GDPR's conditions. You may object, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21), and withdraw consent with future effect where processing is based on consent. Contact the controller above to exercise these rights. Requests about data held by GitHub, Apple, or another provider should be made directly to that provider.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority under Article 77 GDPR, in particular where you live, work, or where an alleged infringement occurred. In North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany, the authority is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia.

8. Required information and automated decisions

You do not need to provide personal data to the developer to use the app. Support information is voluntary, though a request may not be answerable without enough detail. The developer does not make decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

9. Children and changes

NTFS Manager is a general-purpose desktop utility and is not specifically directed at children. This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices materially change. The effective date identifies the current version.
